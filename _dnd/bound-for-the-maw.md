The forest whispers with unease. Long before your capture, rumors had spread of an old church abandoned on the edge of the woods, a place where prayers turned to silence and silence turned to hunger. No town dares claim the ruin now, for something beneath it stirs - a presence that promises power to the desperate and despair to all others. You are not the first to be taken by those who serve it. The question is, will you be the last?
